Approach

Three habits that survive contact with a real deadline.

Start from the constraint
Not from the stack. From the team that has to maintain it, the traffic it must survive, the budget, and the date. Most bad architectures are a good technology answering a problem nobody actually has.
Buy before you build
A managed service is usually the cheaper answer, and the interesting work is the part that is genuinely specific to the problem. The job is knowing which is which, early, before anyone has written code.
Leave a trail
Decisions, not just diagrams. If it cannot be explained in a paragraph, it cannot be changed safely by somebody who was not there when it was built.
